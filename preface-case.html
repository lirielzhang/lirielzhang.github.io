Preface Coffee-and-Learn Campaign Concept

A portfolio campaign concept exploring how a lifestyle-learning space can turn offline curiosity into digital interaction, lead capture and repeat engagement.

This is a public-data-based portfolio case. It does not use internal brand data. Simulated metrics are used only to demonstrate campaign reporting logic.

1. Background & Problem

Brand Observation

Not just coffee, not just AI learning

Preface can be understood as a lifestyle-learning space where coffee, technology learning and community experience overlap.

This positioning is more approachable than a formal course provider and more distinctive than a regular café.

Marketing Problem

The action path can be clearer

The offline space can create curiosity, but the path from discovery to digital action needs to be easier to see and measure.

  • Do users scan a QR code?
  • Do they complete an AI-Q test?
  • Do they leave email or WhatsApp contact?
  • Do they return for an event or workshop?

2. Campaign Idea

Campaign Concept

Take Your AI-Q After Coffee

The campaign turns a casual coffee visit into a small learning interaction. Instead of asking users to commit to a full course immediately, it invites them to take a short AI-Q test, receive a simple learning profile and opt in for future Coffee-and-Learn events.

3. Touchpoint Journey

1Instagram Teaser
Use lifestyle visuals and beginner-friendly AI hooks to introduce the Coffee-and-Learn idea.
Metric: impressions, clicks, CTR
2In-store QR Card
Place a simple QR prompt on table cards, cup sleeves or Tech Dose cards.
Metric: QR scans, landing page visits
3AI-Q Test
Invite users to complete a short test and get a beginner-friendly AI learning profile.
Metric: start_aiq_test, aiq_complete
4Email / WhatsApp Opt-in
Offer AI-Q results, event reminders or beginner AI tips as a value exchange.
Metric: newsletter_signup, whatsapp_opt_in
5Event Follow-up
Send relevant Coffee-and-Learn event invitations and reminders.
Metric: event_signup, repeat engagement

What I would report

I would not only report traffic volume. I would compare channels across the funnel: impressions for awareness, CTR for interest, leads for captured intent and sign-ups for meaningful action.

If Instagram generates high impressions but low leads, I would review the creative, CTA and landing page. If Email has lower reach but stronger sign-up quality, I would use it for follow-up and lead nurturing.

5. What This Project Shows

This case demonstrates campaign thinking, touchpoint planning and basic reporting logic. It supports my fit for entry-level digital marketing and campaign support roles where content, user journey and performance review need to work together.
