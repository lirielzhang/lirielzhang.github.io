Main Case

Slowood Social Content & Digital Marketing Strategy

A public-data-based content strategy project for a Hong Kong sustainable lifestyle retailer.

This project uses public social content, Xiaohongshu search signals, competitor references and user comments to explore how Slowood could make its sustainable lifestyle message more searchable, practical and shareable.

Public-data researchSocial content auditSearch insightKPI framework

Self-initiated portfolio case. Not an official Slowood project. No real campaign results are claimed.

Project overview

Review public social signals and translate them into a practical content activation plan.

Role & skills demonstrated

Social media audit, competitor reference, consumer language coding, content planning and visual reporting.

Research question

How can Slowood turn sustainable lifestyle interest into searchable, save-worthy and repeatable social content?

Visual Evidence

Research and diagnosis visuals

Strategy

From signal to content system

Data sources

Instagram posts, Story Highlights, Xiaohongshu notes, reviews/articles and competitor references.

Core diagnosis

Slowood has strong sustainable lifestyle values, but public user language is often more concrete: what to buy, where to visit, what smells good and what is worth saving.

Strategic direction

Translate brand values into low-barrier actions: starter baskets, scent guides, route prompts and refill routines.

Content pillar

First Slowood Basket

Reduce first-time choice friction with starter baskets by need.

Content pillar

Slowood Scent Finder

Reduce sensory uncertainty through scent mood guides and story polls.

Content pillar

Stop-by Moments

Turn Central, airport and weekend routes into visit occasions.

Content pillar

One Small Swap

Convert sustainability education into repeatable refill and reuse actions.
