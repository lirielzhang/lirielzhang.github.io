Project 01 · Main case

Slowood Social Content Audit & Strategy

A self-initiated portfolio case using public social signals to design content strategy and campaign touchpoints. Not an official Slowood project.

FocusSocial content strategy
DataIG · XHS · Reviews · Benchmarks

Research question

How can Slowood turn existing consumer interest into more save-worthy, participatory and repeatable social content?

Save-worthy

Users keep it for decisions.

Participatory

Users can answer or repost.

Repeatable

Users have a reason to return.

Key findings

Data did not become copied titles. It became content tasks.

01

Audience exists, but participation is weak.

36K followers1,030 posts15 IG posts reviewedNear-zero comments

So what: the opportunity is not only awareness, but participation conversion.

02

Brand voice is educational and value-led.

sustainabilityrefilleco educationstore updates

So what: values are clear, but many posts need stronger response moments.

03

Consumer voice is decision-oriented.

must-buyfragrancesouvenirCentral / airport

So what: consumers approach Slowood through purchase, scent, route and gifting decisions.

04

Shareable assets exist, but the UGC loop is unclear.

aesthetic storesrefill servicescent productsfew UGC prompts

So what: the brand needs a clearer mechanism that turns store experience into participation.

Diagnosis

A content translation gap

Brand Voice Sustainable living · Refill culture · Eco choices · Education carousel · Store updates
Translation Gap

Low visible interaction is not only an engagement issue. It reflects a gap between brand values and consumer decision language.

Consumer Voice What to buy · What smells good · Where to visit · What makes a good gift · What is worth it

Project 02 · Supporting case

Xiaohongshu Headline Performance Analysis

Academic project exploring how high-engagement posts can inform headline optimisation and content brief logic.

RoleData collection · cleaning · headline analysis
ToolsPython · Selenium · Pandas · Requests
OutputHeadline framework · prototype logic

Content-level analysis

From high-engagement posts to reusable headline rules.

Xiaohongshu is a UGC-driven platform where headlines strongly affect whether users click, save or interact. This project selected high-performing public posts and translated them into headline generation logic.

Mock post card

香港本地人也爱的茶餐厅，猪排多士外脆里嫩！

Likes · saves · comments as engagement signals

Data process

CollectCleanRankSelectBuild Prompt

Collect public post data with Python, Selenium and Requests.

Clean duplicates, emojis, garbled text and irrelevant fields with Pandas.

Rank posts by likes and favourites as engagement signals.

Select high-quality examples for prompt design.

Selection logic

Not only high numbers.

I also considered whether each example could teach the prototype a useful headline pattern.

High likes & favourites Attractive headline Title-content diversity Long enough content HK food / travel tips Topic hashtags removed

Headline framework

Useful headline = clear benefit + specific context + matched content

Benefit

Users quickly know what they can eat, buy, visit, save, avoid or learn.

Specificity

Location, price, identity and scenario make a post easier to judge.

Content fit

A good headline attracts attention without misleading the reader.
