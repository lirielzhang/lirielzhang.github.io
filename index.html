Why this case

From existing interest to visible participation

Slowood has clear brand assets: a sustainable lifestyle positioning, multiple store touchpoints, refill services, aesthetic retail spaces and a recognisable product mix. This makes it a useful case to explore how social content can turn existing consumer interest into saves, responses, store actions and repeat participation.

Research Question

How can Slowood turn existing consumer interest into more save-worthy, participatory and repeatable social content?

Repeatable

Key findings

Four findings shaped the strategy

Finding 01

Audience exists, but visible participation is weak.

36K followers1,030 posts15 IG posts reviewedNear-zero comments

Evidence: Slowood has 36K IG followers and 1,030 posts. In the reviewed 15-post sample, visible comments were mostly near zero.

So what: The opportunity is not only awareness growth, but participation conversion.

Finding 02

Brand voice is educational and value-led.

SustainabilityRefillEco educationStore updates

Evidence: Brand content leans toward sustainability, refill, education and product/service information.

So what: The content communicates values clearly, but needs stronger action and response prompts.

Finding 03

Consumer voice is concrete and decision-oriented.

Must-buyFragranceSouvenirCentral / airport

Evidence: XHS and review signals include must-buy, fragrance, souvenir, Central / airport, price and avoid-buying hooks.

So what: Consumers often approach Slowood through purchase, visit, scent and gifting decisions.

Finding 04

Offline assets are shareable, but the UGC loop is not clear.

Aesthetic storesRefill serviceScent productsFew UGC prompts

Evidence: Stores, refill, scent products and gift scenarios are naturally shareable, while Highlights are mainly informational.

So what: Slowood needs a clearer mechanism that turns store experience into user participation.

Diagnosis

A content translation gap

Brand Voice

  • Sustainable living
  • Refill culture
  • Eco choices
  • Education carousel
  • Product / service updates
Translation Gap

Low visible interaction is not only an engagement issue. It reflects a gap between brand values and consumer decision language.

Consumer Voice

  • Must-buy / gift ideas
  • Fragrance / incense
  • Central / airport visit
  • Price / value judgement
  • Avoid-buying review

The opportunity is to translate Slowood’s sustainable lifestyle positioning into content that is easier to save, answer, share and repeat.

Audience segments

Two priority audiences for junior social content planning

Segment A

First-time visitors & gift shoppers

Motivation: They want to know where to go, what to buy, what is gift-safe and what is worth saving before a Hong Kong trip or store visit.

Triggers: must-buy, souvenir, hidden gem, airport, Central, price/value.

Content need: starter baskets, gift-safe picks, stop-by moments, expectation-setting guides.

Segment B

Local lifestyle & scent/refill regulars

Motivation: They are attracted by fragrance, incense, home routine, refill and Slowood’s lifestyle tone.

Triggers: scent mood, routine, refill first step, reuse, repeat purchase.

Content need: scent finder, first refill guide, one small swap, UGC story template.

Strategy

Start Small with Slowood

The strategy reframes Slowood’s sustainable lifestyle positioning into low-barrier actions: choose one product, test one scent, save one visit moment, and begin one refill step.

Project 02 · Academic Project

Xiaohongshu Headline Performance Analysis

Academic project exploring how high-engagement posts can inform headline optimisation and content brief logic.

Content analyticsHeadline qualityPrototype logicSocial media writing

Project overview

From high-engagement posts to headline rules

Xiaohongshu is a UGC-driven platform where headlines strongly affect whether users click, save or interact with a post. This academic project focused on identifying useful headline patterns from high-engagement posts and turning them into a headline generation prototype.

Our group studied public Xiaohongshu post data and selected high-performing examples to build a simple headline generation prototype. Users input post content, and the tool generates several Xiaohongshu-style headline options.

Project overview

What this supporting case proves

Project goal

Identify useful headline patterns from high-engagement Xiaohongshu posts and turn them into prototype logic.

My focus

Sample selection criteria

I did not only look at engagement numbers.

I also considered whether each example could teach the prototype a useful headline pattern.

High likes & favourites

Indicates stronger audience response.

Attractive headline

Shows a clear content hook.

Title-content diversity

Avoids one-style output.

Long enough post content

Provides enough context for generation.

HK food / travel tips

Matches local recommendation content.

Topic hashtags removed

Keeps focus on headline wording.

Headline quality framework

What makes a useful Xiaohongshu headline?

Useful Headline

Clear benefit + Specific context + Matched content

Benefit

Users should quickly know what they can eat, buy, visit, save, avoid or learn.

Specificity

Location, price, identity and scenario make a post easier to judge.

Content Fit

A good headline attracts attention without misleading the reader.
