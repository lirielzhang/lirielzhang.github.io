Technical work was kept simple in the portfolio: the value is not the crawler itself, but how engagement signals and headline features become content rules.

香港本地人也爱的茶餐厅，猪排多士外脆里嫩！

LikesSavesComments

Headline framework

Useful headline = clear benefit + specific context + matched content

Benefit

Users quickly know what they can eat, buy, visit, save, avoid or learn.

Specific context

Location, price, identity and scenario make a post easier to judge.

Matched content

A strong hook should attract attention without misleading the reader.

Input content

今天中午特意带小伙伴们来打卡一家我爱吃的茶餐厅。这家【炳记茶档】真的是香港排队王，好多艺人同行也很爱来打卡。我推荐的第一名就是【猪排多士】，外面脆脆的，里面的肉嫩嫩的有嚼劲。如果你们来香港一定要来试试。

Generated-style headline options
  1. 来香港一定要吃的茶餐厅！炳记猪排多士真的香
  2. 香港排队王茶档打卡｜猪排多士值得专门来吃吗？
  3. 第一次来香港别错过这家茶餐厅，猪排多士太上头
  4. 香港本地人也爱的茶餐厅，猪排多士外脆里嫩！

Sample output based on original academic prototype logic. No live API connection is claimed.

How I would validate it

A/B test plan, not fake results.

Test

Compare benefit-led vs curiosity-led headline options.

Metrics

Click-through, saves, comments, and title-content fit feedback.

Decision

Keep headline rules that improve action without misleading readers.

Case 02 · Social content strategy

Slowood Social Content Audit & Strategy

A self-initiated portfolio case based on public social signals. The goal is not to invent a campaign from nowhere, but to show how data can shape content tasks and campaign touchpoints.

36K IG followers 1,030 IG posts 15 IG posts reviewed 15 XHS notes coded

What I found

Research summary

Instagram

Content is mainly educational; visible comments in the sample are mostly near zero.

Xiaohongshu

Users search through must-buy, scent, souvenir, Central / airport and worth-it language.

Reviews & articles

Store experience, price/value, refill friction and product choice are repeated decision points.

Benchmarks

Sustainable content works better when it becomes practical, routine-based and easy to imitate.

Core diagnosis

Brand vs. consumer language mismatch

Brand says
  • Sustainable living
  • Refill culture
  • Eco education
  • Store updates
Mismatch
Consumers ask
  • What should I buy?
  • What smells good?
  • Is it worth it?
  • When should I visit?

The opportunity is to translate Slowood’s sustainable lifestyle positioning into content that is easier to save, answer, share and repeat.

Strategy

Start Small with Slowood

Translate sustainability into low-barrier actions: choose one product, test one scent, save one visit moment, and begin one refill step.
