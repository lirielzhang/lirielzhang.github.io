Preface Coffee-and-Learn Digital Journey Audit

A public-data-based portfolio case study exploring how a lifestyle-learning space can connect offline interest with digital interaction, lead capture and repeat engagement.

This is not an internal brand project. The case is based on public-facing brand observations, digital journey thinking and simulated reporting data to demonstrate my approach to digital marketing analysis.

1. Case Summary

Project Scope

What this case examines

The case looks at how Preface, as a coffee-and-learn lifestyle space, could turn casual offline and social interest into trackable digital actions.

  • Brand context: coffee, learning, AI and community experience
  • Focus: offline-to-digital journey and lead capture
  • Output: journey map, strategy modules, GA4-style measurement plan and dashboard logic
Role & Skills

My role in the case

  • Public-facing brand observation
  • Customer journey mapping
  • Content and CRM touchpoint planning
  • GA4-style event and KPI thinking
  • Dashboard structure for campaign reporting

2. Core Diagnosis

The main gap is not awareness alone. It is the path after discovery.

Preface has strong lifestyle and learning assets, but the user journey from casual interest to measurable action can be clearer.

The key question is not only whether users notice the space. The more important question is: after they discover or visit Preface, do they take a trackable next step?

  • Do users scan a QR code?
  • Do they complete an AI-Q test?
  • Do they leave an email or WhatsApp contact?
  • Do they receive a follow-up?
  • Do they return for another event or workshop?

3. Coffee-and-Learn Journey

The proposed journey connects discovery, in-store experience, digital interaction and CRM follow-up.

1. Online DiscoveryIG, search, social content
User sees coffee-and-learn content or AI-related event information.
Brand highlights lifestyle, learning and community hooks.
Metrics:
Impressions, clicks, CTR
2. In-store ExperienceCoffee, space, event atmosphere
User visits the space and notices tech-learning touchpoints.
Brand uses table cards, cup sleeves or Tech Dose cards.
Metrics:
QR scans, CTA clicks
3. Micro-learning TouchpointAI-Q / short quiz
User starts a low-friction AI-Q interaction.
Brand makes learning feel short, accessible and shareable.
Metrics:
start_aiq_test, completion rate
4. Lead CaptureEmail / WhatsApp opt-in
User leaves contact information to receive results or reminders.
Brand offers AI-Q results, beginner tips or event updates.
Metrics:
newsletter_signup, whatsapp_opt_in
5. CRM Follow-upEmail, WhatsApp, newsletter
User receives relevant content or workshop invitations.
Brand sends segmented follow-up based on user interest.
Metrics:
open rate, click rate, event_signup
6. Repeat EngagementCommunity and learning loop
User returns for another event, guide or learning activity.
Brand builds repeat participation and community connection.
Metrics:
returning users, repeat sign-ups

4. Strategy Modules

In-store QR Touchpoints

Make the next step visible

Use table cards, cup sleeves or Tech Dose cards to guide users to a simple AI-Q test or event landing page.

AI-Q as Micro-learning Hook

Turn curiosity into action

A short quiz gives visitors a low-friction reason to interact with the brand digitally after discovering the offline space.

CRM Opt-in

Capture interest with a clear value exchange

Offer AI-Q results, beginner learning tips or event reminders in exchange for email or WhatsApp opt-in.

Follow-up Content

Move users toward repeat engagement

Send beginner-friendly learning content, event reminders and workshop recommendations based on user interest.

6. Dashboard Logic

What a simple marketing dashboard should show

A dashboard for this case should not only show traffic. It should connect channel performance with lead capture and repeat engagement.

Awareness
Impressions

How many times the campaign is seen.

Interest
CTR

Whether people are interested enough to click.

Lead Capture
Opt-in Rate

Whether users leave email or WhatsApp contact.

Final Action
Sign-ups

Whether users register for an event or workshop.

Channel Performance

Compare sources

  • Sessions, clicks, leads and sign-ups by source / medium
  • Instagram for discovery and awareness
  • Email for follow-up and lead nurturing
  • Search for users with stronger intent
Funnel View

Find the weak step

  • QR scan → AI-Q start → AI-Q complete → opt-in → event sign-up
  • If CTR is low: review creative, copy and targeting
  • If clicks are high but leads are low: review landing page, CTA and form
  • If leads are high but sign-ups are low: review follow-up and event value
Content Performance

Review engagement

  • Top posts or pages by engagement and CTA clicks
  • Content themes that drive stronger interaction
  • Potential A/B testing for headlines, visuals and CTAs
CRM Performance

Track follow-up quality

  • Email open rate and click rate
  • WhatsApp opt-in and event reminder response
  • Repeat event participation or returning users

What This Case Demonstrates

This case demonstrates my ability to connect content, user journey, CRM thinking and basic measurement logic. Rather than treating marketing as only content production, I used a funnel-based approach to ask: where do users come from, what actions do they take, and how can the brand guide them toward more meaningful engagement?
