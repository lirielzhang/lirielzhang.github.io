A self-initiated portfolio case exploring how Preface could make AI learning feel lighter, more social and easier to start.

This is a self-initiated portfolio case based on public-facing brand observation. It does not use internal brand data.

Audience Insight

AI feels useful, but the first step can feel unclear.

The campaign focuses on AI-curious young professionals in Hong Kong who are interested in AI for work and daily life, but may not feel ready to join a full course immediately.

BarrierUser ThoughtCampaign Response
Feels technical“I am not from a technical background.”Myth-busting: AI is not only for coders
No clear first step“I do not know where to start.”AI-Q: find your beginner starting point
Too much commitment“I do not want to join a full course yet.”2-minute test + one small tool tip
Lacks social context“Learning alone feels boring.”Coffee-and-Learn session

Campaign Strategy

Make AI learning feel smaller, lighter and easier to start.

The core message is simple: users do not need to master AI overnight. They can start with one small question, one useful tool or one Coffee-and-Learn session.

Audience needA low-pressure first step into AI learning.
Campaign ideaStart AI Small.
Action pathSocial content -> AI-Q -> opt-in -> Coffee-and-Learn.

Touchpoint Journey

From social content to event participation.

01

Social Post

User action: Sees beginner-friendly AI content.

Brand action: Introduces a small, approachable AI learning idea.

Metric: Reach, saves, clicks.

02

AI-Q Landing Page

User action: Clicks or scans a QR code.

Brand action: Explains the value of a short AI-Q test.

Metric: CTR, landing page visits, AI-Q starts.

03

Result + Opt-in

User action: Completes AI-Q and leaves email or WhatsApp.

Brand action: Gives a beginner profile and useful next step.

Metric: Completion rate, opt-ins.

04

WhatsApp / Email Follow-up

User action: Opens tips or event reminders.

Brand action: Sends beginner tips and Coffee-and-Learn invitation.

Metric: Open rate, click rate.

05

Coffee-and-Learn Event

User action: Signs up or attends.

Brand action: Provides a casual learning experience.

Metric: Sign-ups, attendance.

06

Post-event Recap

User action: Receives recap and next learning step.

Brand action: Encourages continued engagement.

Metric: Repeat sign-ups, email clicks.

Reporting & Optimisation

Using basic signals to decide what to improve.

Data SignalMeaningOptimisation
High impressions, low CTRContent is seen but not attractive enoughTest headline, creative and CTA
High CTR, low AI-Q startsLanding page or value proposition is unclearMake AI-Q benefit clearer and reduce friction
High AI-Q starts, low completionTest may feel too long or boringShorten questions and add progress bar
High completion, low opt-inValue exchange is weakOffer personalised result or event reminder
High opt-in, low sign-upFollow-up does not persuade actionImprove event message, timing and reminder
